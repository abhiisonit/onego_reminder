Exact Android Alarms

Scheduled using Android AlarmManager to trigger precisely on time, waking up seamlessly even during Android Doze battery optimizations.

🔄

How to Install on Android

Follow these 3 quick steps to sideload the APK onto your device.

1

Download the APK

Click the Download APK button above to download reminders.apk directly to your Android device.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

When prompted, tap Settings and toggle Allow from this source for your browser or file manager.

3

Install and Open

Tap Install, launch Reminders, and tap Allow when asked for notification permissions so you never miss an alert.
